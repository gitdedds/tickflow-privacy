TickFlow Privacy Policy

Your thoughts stay yours.

Last updated: November 2026

The short version: TickFlow does not collect, sell, track, or share your personal data. There are no user accounts, no third-party analytics SDKs, and no advertising trackers. Everything you create lives privately on your device and in your personal Apple iCloud account.

1. Data Storage & iCloud Sync

All tasks, micro-steps, routine stacks, Dopamine Menu items, and app preferences are stored locally on your Apple device. If you have iCloud enabled, TickFlow uses Apple's private CloudKit database and iCloud Key-Value Storage to sync your data securely across your own iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

We do not operate external servers and have zero access to your tasks, notes, or iCloud container.

2. Permissions You Can Optionally Grant

TickFlow only requests device permissions when you actively use a feature that needs them:

  • Microphone & Speech Recognition (Voice Brain Dump): Used solely to transcribe your spoken thoughts into tasks. On supported devices, transcription runs entirely on-device. Audio is never recorded to disk or sent to TickFlow servers.
  • Apple Calendar: Used optionally to display your upcoming events inside TickFlow's "Next on Calendar" runway, trigger gentle Transition Warnings, and export dated tasks when you enable two-way sync.
  • Notifications: Used locally on your device for task reminders, transition heads-ups, and the optional "No ADHD Tax" 2-day free trial renewal reminder.

3. On-Device Intelligence

Features such as Magic Breakdown run locally using Apple's on-device Foundation Models (where available) or TickFlow's built-in offline templates. Your task text is never sent to external third-party AI servers.

4. Purchases & Subscriptions

TickFlow Pro subscriptions and Lifetime Passes are processed exclusively by Apple through the App Store (StoreKit 2). TickFlow never sees or stores your payment card details, billing address, or Apple Account credentials.

5. Children's Privacy

Because TickFlow does not collect any personal information from any user, it complies with children's privacy regulations worldwide, including COPPA and UK GDPR.

6. Deleting Your Data

You are always in complete control of your data. You can delete individual tasks or clear your archive inside the app at any time, or remove all data permanently by deleting TickFlow from your devices and clearing TickFlow's data in your iCloud Settings.

7. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or TickFlow, please get in touch at support@tickflow.app.
