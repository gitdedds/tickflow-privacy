Support & Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers for getting the most out of TickFlow—and a direct line if you ever need help.

How do I map my iPhone Action Button to Voice Brain Dump?

Open iPhone Settings → Action Button, swipe to Shortcut, tap Choose a Shortcut, search for TickFlow, and select Voice Brain Dump (or Start 2-Minute Micro-Sprint).

How does iCloud Sync work across iPhone, iPad, and Mac?

TickFlow syncs automatically through your personal Apple iCloud account using private CloudKit storage. Just make sure you are signed into the same Apple Account on each device with iCloud Drive enabled—no separate TickFlow login is needed.

How do I restore my TickFlow Pro purchase or manage my subscription?

Open Lists & Brain → Settings (slider icon, top right) → TickFlow Pro and tap Restore Purchases. You can cancel or change any App Store subscription anytime in iPhone Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions.

Is my voice or task data sent to external servers?

No. Voice Brain Dump uses Apple's on-device speech recognition where supported, Magic Breakdown runs locally, and your tasks are stored only on your devices and your private iCloud container. Read our full Privacy Policy for details.
